Hero models · for review · not linked from the site

Ways to open AKAI

Each model is a full page you can open, resize and try. Pick one (or mix ideas) and it replaces the current hero.

01 Page and pixel

A bone page on black with the name cropped by its edge. The K and A start as scattered dots, drift, then assemble; the cursor pushes them away and a click shatters them.

02 Projector

All black. The name is faint everywhere and fully lit only under a beam that follows the cursor.

03 Departure board

Four split-flap tiles spin through the alphabet and land on AKAI, echoing the Santiago clock.

04 Split

The name stood on its side in heavy type on one half; the serif argument and the four disciplines on the other.

Round 2 · different directions

05 Prompt

The hero is a question: “What do you want to create?” Pick a chip or type, and Send opens the email with it written. A form, not a chatbot.

06 Portal

Your own icon as a window onto two dot grids drifting against each other; the moiré moves with the pointer.

07 Sentence

One serif sentence where the middle word keeps changing: brands, websites, platforms, automations, experiences. No name poster.

08 Blueprint

The name is drawn like a technical drawing: guides, then the outline, then it fills. The width in the margin is measured from the drawing.

Round 3 · a fusion

09 Sentence on a blueprint

Models 7 and 8 together: “We build ___” where each word is drawn as an outline over the real guides of the typeface (ascender, x-height, baseline, descender), fills in, and is measured.

Round 4 · with the portal mark

10 Sentence on a blueprint + portal mark

Model 9 with your icon from model 6 in the top-left corner instead of the word AKAI: a small window onto drifting dots.
