Hardware store system

What we built

  • A point of sale with product search, barcode scanning, quotes and held sales
  • Inventory, purchasing, cash register, dispatch and delivery routes
  • Accounts receivable and payable, expenses and accounting
  • Electronic invoice (e-CF) records, credit notes and printed receipts
  • Four AI assistants, one each for sales, purchasing, inventory and finance, inside a chat in the system
