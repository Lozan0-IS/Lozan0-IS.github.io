THE AKAI METHOD

/02
  1. 01 THINK

    Creativity defines the idea.

    We start with what you are trying to create, not with which technology to use.

  2. 02 SHAPE

    Design gives it form.

    Identity, interface and interaction, decided together so the result reads as one thing.

  3. 03 BUILD

    Technology makes it possible.

    We choose the stack for the problem, and keep it out of the way of the experience.

  4. 04 MOVE

    Automation makes it scale.

    We launch, then turn what repeats into systems, so the work keeps running without you.
